# Saved city statistics and briefs

The statistics launcher reads saved, count-only assets. Opening it, dragging the eagle, selecting a city or changing the brief period never calls a model. A site's accepted artifact may include the launcher by building with `VITE_SAVED_STATISTICS=true`; ordinary source-only CI and builds keep it disabled when those assets are absent.

The artifact supplies `statistics/manifest.json` with schema version 1, its lowercase `city`, exact native `map_generation`, `map_announcements`, the checked source `mapping_sha256`, and a boolean `static_reports_checked`. It also supplies `content-tag-summary.json`, `macro-tag-summary.json`, `window-summary.json` and `cached-briefs.json` under `statistics/`. Count, city, generation and mapping bindings must match before display; each catalogue's existing validators require the 14 city scopes and their correctly summed total. Optional checked Chinese reports use `statistics/static-reports/{city-or-all14}.html` and become links only when `static_reports_checked` is true.

These files are generated release artifacts, kept outside code Git. Copy only the reviewed, explicitly hash-bound public asset set. Private source narratives, evidence overlays, provenance, model responses, job logs/state, credentials and preview fixtures never enter the site. The artifact gate accepts explicit `script-src 'none'` for script-free reports while retaining its existing executable-content, path, credential, privacy and size checks.

The primary layer has eight macro labels and four hate facets; twenty ordinary offence/conduct types remain collapsed. Each table separately shows supported, no-support, uncertain and not-evaluated records. A label's content index is `100 × supported records / all selected records`; uncertain and not-evaluated records stay in the denominator and are not counted as supported. A zero denominator makes the index unavailable. Evaluation coverage is reported separately. New batches that have not passed their source and label checks do not silently replace the accepted snapshot or its cutoff date.

Counts describe selected announcements, including unknown locations, and do not establish city crime risk or the number of unique crimes. Source-backed narrative leads remain distinct from police wording and court findings. No composite index is calculated unless weights are explicitly supplied and disclosed.

Brief periods use German local announcement publication dates. A missing date is excluded from those periods and remains visible in overall counts; incident dates and inferred dates are not substitutes. Saved partial briefs and incomplete labels stay clearly marked. The offline, owner-operated generation process is separate from this browser UI; no visitor-accessible model endpoint is included. Final native mounting, repository BASE_URL, mobile/device checks, full language/label coverage and release acceptance remain separate requirements.
